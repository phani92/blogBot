### A Manifesto for Algorithmic Resiliency

To stabilize the global economy against continuous systemic shocks, global financial architecture must shift from reactive speculation to structural resilience.

1. **Mandate Dynamic Entropy Pricing:** Credit agencies and financial clearinghouses must integrate real-time socio-political, health, and security metrics into asset risk models, moving beyond backward-looking financial metrics.
2. **Decentralize Critical Physical Infrastructure:** As energy grid failures in war zones and localized transit shocks demonstrate, capital must prioritize decentralized power, localized manufacturing, and redundant logistics over hyper-optimized, single-point-of-failure efficiency.
3. **Deprecate Single-Point-of-Failure Governance:** Economic systems must insulate international trade and market operations from the sudden physical incapacity or political realignment of individual human actors.

Until global capital learns to measure and price the structural fragility of the human bedrock underneath it, market stability will remain an expensive illusion.
